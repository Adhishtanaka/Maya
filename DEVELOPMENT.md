## Limits to keep visible

- Forty-four persistent residents, lightweight family/club actors, walking police and sixty-four building addresses with two interior floors are implemented; this is not an authored simulation of every inhabitant or room in a real metropolis.
- Police vehicles and emergency crews use simplified collision-aware navigation and service behaviors. They do not implement a forensic investigation model or a complete tactical combat AI.
- The first-person view shares the same underlying ground-plane aiming/collision simulation as the elevated view. There is no full body-part ballistics model.
- Weather and hill regions are atmospheric; steep hill meshes are solid obstacles rather than traversable elevation terrain.
- Property income and scene cleanup run while the game is active. Menus pause simulation.
- Browser local storage provides an autosave plus three manual snapshots. Loading a snapshot also keeps the prior autosave under `maya-before-load` for recovery. Cloud saves, accounts and multiplayer are not implemented.
- Faction districts track influence from completed jobs. This is not yet an autonomous gang-war simulation. The Harbor Rivals event has two AI opponents; the other three events are timed runs.
- Roadblocks and traffic can create congestion. Ambulance crews can walk the final approach when nearby traffic blocks their vehicle. Full traffic-jam recovery remains an area for longer-session testing.
- A successful production build is not a production-readiness sign-off. Target-device performance, long-session stability, content completeness and balancing still need dedicated QA.

## Continuation: 0.9.0

- Six vehicle models: classic, compact, sports coupe, SUV, cargo van and three-wheeler. Their mass, acceleration, top speed and steering differ. Cabins have transparent windows, seats, visible occupants, spinning wheels and hinged doors.
- Traffic drivers are existing named residents, not duplicate anonymous people. They leave a car after finding a clear kerb, then resume their schedule. Theft includes approach, door opening, driver exit and player seating; blocked exits and moving vehicles reject entry. The player’s seated model replaces the former driver.
- Collision impulses conserve momentum at contact, dissipate energy and apply lateral motion and spin. Stationary vehicles can be pushed; people receive decaying knockback. Critical vehicle health starts a five-second fire warning; zero health detonates immediately. Explosions damage nearby unobstructed people and cars, may chain, and leave a charred wreck. Repair cancels burning; saved wrecks do not explode again on reload.
- Serious player/police vehicle impacts trigger an armed dismount even when the player remains in the car. Officers fire visible tracers; car occupants take reduced bullet damage and their vehicle also takes damage. Four additional named adult crew members have their own jobs, biographies, dialogue and schedules, carry weapons, retaliate to nearby violence and drop ammunition-bearing weapons on incapacitation. Police also drop their sidearms.
- Adult character variants add different silhouettes, heights, hairstyles, jackets and skirts, with work/casual wardrobe changes. Hip/shoulder pivots, knees and elbows support walking, sprinting and seated poses. Static model pieces sharing materials are merged to reduce draw calls.
- De Silva Motors offers six vehicle purchases and repairs. Purchases, models and colors persist. Amma’s food menu has three health/stamina options; the existing gun shop remains available. Full bays, insufficient funds and the garage capacity prevent invalid purchases.
- Compact HUD, freely moving mouse look in close views, a chase camera that follows the car and recenters after looking around, a more angled elevated view, and an upright-camera fix for south-facing views. Touch look remains available.
- New unit and browser checks cover mass/energy response, glancing impulses, burning/repair/explosion lifecycle, animated theft, armed police against a driver, gang drops, dealership persistence, food purchases and upright views in every compass direction.

This is still a stylized lightweight simulation. Physics uses ground-plane collision volumes and impulses, not a full suspension or deformable-body solver. Drivers currently complete one circuit trip before returning to pedestrian routines; full recurring household vehicle ownership/commuting is not implemented. Crew members react locally rather than running autonomous territory wars. Character animation is procedural, not motion capture. Production device profiling, art refinement and longer traffic/combat soak testing remain open.

## Continuation: 0.10.0

- Nine road vehicle models, including new bicycle, motorcycle and bus geometry; the existing cargo van remains available. Each has its own size, mass, acceleration, steering and speed. Two-wheelers have seated riders and animated wheels/pedaling. Narrower collision footprints fit smaller gaps, and long buses use longer front/rear clearance. A destroyed bicycle stops without exploding.
- The marked northern Air & Cycle Depot sells finite ordnance, jetpacks, parachutes and fuel, and charters a utility helicopter and survey plane. Both aircraft are player-controlled, with altitude-aware static collisions. Planes need forward speed to take off and lose height when too slow. Helicopters rise vertically. Land before leaving an aircraft, or bail out with a parachute. Abandoned aircraft descend and become wrecks. Aircraft access persists; positions/damage/fuel do not.
- Jetpacks use finite fuel except with the session-only SKYHIGH modifier. Parachutes need five meters of height, descend steadily, and avoid freefall landing damage. Cameras follow altitude without accumulating height, and airborne players no longer block or collide with traffic at street level. Touch flight controls provide ascent, descent, missile and parachute actions.
- Grenades arc, bounce and explode after 2.5 seconds. C4 stays placed until remotely triggered on the same interior floor; it requires ground placement. Missiles use swept movement and explode on scenery/ground impact or timeout. Ammunition is bounded, bursts are rate-limited, effects expire and walls block blast damage. Explosives can damage vehicles and nearby adult combat actors. Bullets use reduced base damage (pistol 24, carbine 20) with distance falloff; healthy targets require multiple hits.
- All 64 building addresses have separate ground and upper scenes, constructed lazily. Upper templates add divided rooms, a bed, shelves, office and kitchen furnishings. Each address has a building-type work order: read it downstairs, perform two timed upstairs steps, then receive Rs. 220 once. - Ceylon Social Club has a bar, DJ desk, stage, seating, lights and four explicitly adult performers with individual biographies/dialogue. Performances and three original music patterns run from 18:00 to 04:00. Three named children and one guardian have a separate noncombat school/park/home routine, student clothes and dialogue. These background actors are separate from the 44 persistent combat residents.
- Weapon drops now retain their resident source and floor. Disarmed residents retain that state in saves, and upper-floor interactions cannot collect a ground-floor drop. Jetpack/parachute strafing follows the camera at every compass heading; stationary bicycles and motorcycles do not pedal.
- Equipment and cheat menus expose RUNFAST, SKYHIGH, AIRFLEET, ARMORY, POCKETS, PEACE and ARMOR. Movement/fuel/invulnerability modifiers reset on reload. Equipment, aircraft access and cash use the existing save format with bounded migration.
- The provided elephant emblem sets the UI/map palette: ivory #faf7ee, charcoal #383536, burgundy #a62646 and olive #919947. Welcome branding, SVG/PNG favicon, Apple touch icon, manifest, page description and social-card image/metadata are included. The share image/icons are generated from the SVG by `scripts/brand-assets.mjs`. Public-domain canonical/absolute social URLs remain deployment configuration; offline caching is not implemented.

Remaining production depth: flight uses simplified controls/body clearance rather than aerodynamic simulation, rotor/wing collision volumes or a complete airport system. Upper floors are scene transitions, not walkable stair meshes. Furniture/work orders share templates. School and club actors use lightweight routines rather than full households and recurring story arcs. Dynamic vehicle deformation, advanced suspension, autonomous gang wars, swimming, authored cinematic animation, long-session stability and representative-device profiling remain open. ## Traffic, police and death recovery fixes — 2026-09-22

- Traffic uses distinct spawn positions and consistent directional lanes. Drivers account for vehicle length when braking, keep clearance behind stopped vehicles, and finish corners without skipping their route targets. Empty traffic routes stop safely.
- Police and ambulance steering is bounded and checked against scenery. Service vehicles brake for traffic, continue through straight route points, and check behind them before reversing. A queue that does not move for six seconds triggers a route recovery attempt instead of waiting forever.
- Disembarked patrol officers walk back to a car before the seated driver reappears. Dismounting clears stale walking routes, and stealing a patrol car no longer teleports an unrelated neighborhood officer to the theft.
- Zero health shows a full-screen WASTED overlay over a grayscale scene for three active seconds. Controls are locked during the sequence; hospital recovery clears movement and police aggression, restores health, and charges treatment once. Death cannot turn into an arrest or overwrite the save with zero health.

The browser test command includes the new death suite.
